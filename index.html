College Bibliography

Welcome to our college's recommended reading list. These books are essential for students across various disciplines.

Book 1: The Elements of Style

Author: William Strunk Jr. and E.B. White
This classic guide offers timeless advice on clear and concise writing.
Perfect for students aiming to improve their academic writing skills.

Book 2: Thinking, Fast and Slow

Author: Daniel Kahneman
A deep dive into human decision-making and cognitive biases.
Recommended for psychology and business students.

Book 3: Introduction to Algorithms

Author: Thomas H. Cormen et al.
A comprehensive resource for computer science students.
Covers fundamental algorithms and data structures.
